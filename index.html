About

I'm a postdoctoral researcher in the Smart Sensing Systems (S4) Lab at Stanford, where I work with Prof. Zerina Kapetanovic.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from the University of Washington. I was advised by Vikram Iyer and also worked in Sawyer Fuller's Autonomous Insect Robotics Lab. I also earned my B.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering at UW, where I worked on origami structures with Jinkyu Yang.

My research interests are low-power systems, small-scale robotics, computing for sustainability, and applied machine learning.

Outreach

AVELA: A Vision for Engineering Literacy & Access

6,000+students taught
110,000+hours of instruction
500+volunteer members

I co-founded AVELA, a nonprofit that teaches STEM to Black, Latine and other underrepresented students in the Seattle area. I also serve as its board treasurer.

We run workshops, camps and mentoring programs with about 100 partner faculty, schools and community groups, including the City of Seattle, Seattle Public Schools, the Urban League and WA MESA. Our funding comes from Amazon Future Engineers, Apple, SPEEA and BECU.
